# Surface: the phone page (`routes/_phone.tsx`)

Scope: the persistent page around the 3D Nokia 3310 on every phone route. Mode: Experience.
Audience: nostalgia and general visitors who want to play with a 3310. Job: be pressing keys
within seconds, learn the controls, see what the phone is doing, find whose phone it is.
Constraints: the phone stage mounts once and never remounts; the surround never steals the
phone's keys; content is SAMPLE DATA in `content/site.ts`, never embellished.

## Direction contract

FORM: seed:66c9ab31456a76f1 (chosen: own grounded direction "Left on the desk, 2001").

THESIS: the phone has just been put down on a teenager's desk in 2001, and everything you need
to use it is the stuff lying around it. Refuses the bare phone-on-a-void and the portfolio
header/footer frame.

OWN-WORLD: warm wood desk top, paper objects (yellow sticky note, torn lined notebook strip,
SMS slang card, prepaid top-up card), a silver cassette Walkman with orange slide switches, a
navy pencil case with masking tape and correction-pen writing, a biro. Biro-blue Kalam
handwriting, Reenie Beanie scrawls, red-pen hints. One window light from the top left, every
shadow down-right, soft and offset.

STORY: pick up the phone (it is the biggest thing on the desk), the sticky note tells you how,
the notebook copies what the screen says, the pencil case tells you whose desk this is.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the phone ~77% of viewport height, dead centre; sticky note and notebook strip
left, Walkman and pencil case right; all four readable without scrolling.

SIGNATURE: the notebook strip re-copies the LCD by hand (ink wipe) on every screen change; the
sticky note's red "now:" line follows the phone's state.

RISK: props crowd mid widths; handwriting must stay legible (contrast, size) and every prop
that carries information must remain a real control or text, not a picture.
