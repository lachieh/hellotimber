Stock 3310 firmware, v6.39

VIBRA

    Keyboard: arrows ▲/▼, Enter Navi, Backspace or Escape C (hold for standby), digits, * and #, P power (held for 1 s). In Snake II the digits steer and Enter or Escape suspend. The phone starts switched off.

    Settings persist in this page's localStorage. Erase removes every nokia-os/ record and restarts the virgin phone. With Phone security On, a SIM the phone has not seen asks for the security code (12345) at power-on. The virgin phone is nearly silent (its tone settings are unset); Factory settings restarts it over the factory state, where keys beep, tone lists preview and Snake II sounds. Sound starts with the first key or click.
